# ADR 0019 — Seller payouts, VAT invoices and transfer tracker (Sprint 7b)

**Status:** Accepted

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Rationale                             | Alternative considered |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Payout figures in cents with half-up rounding. **Commission sale:** fee = price × fee %, VAT on the fee at the configured rate (the existing quotation `vat_rate_bps`, one source of truth), seller receives price − fee − VAT. **Thabekhulu-renovated:** seller receives the fixed seller price; no fee invoice (Thabekhulu's margin is not a fee). VAT treatment to be confirmed by Thabekhulu's accountant (S7) | PRD money rules, auditable arithmetic | Manual amounts         |
| Payouts only after settlement completes: one finance user prepares (payee and bank reference entered, since Phase 1 listings store no seller identity), a **different** user approves, and payment is recorded with the bank's confirmation (encrypted) (S6)                                                                                                                                                       | Four eyes on every payout             | Single approver        |
| Platform-fee tax invoices to the seller with **gap-free numbers** `TDG-INV-YYYY-NNNN` under a row lock, one per sale, letterhead and VAT number from settings, optional seller VAT number validated (10 digits starting with 4); A4 PDF (S7)                                                                                                                                                                       | Valid, sequential tax invoices        | Free-form invoices     |
| Transfer stages after completion: lodged at the Deeds Office, registered; Mining instead: section 11 consent applied for, granted, transfer registered; shown to staff and to the buyer under Purchases (S8)                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Buyers can follow their transfer      | Notes only             |
