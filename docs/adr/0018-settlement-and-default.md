# ADR 0018 — Settlement and default (Sprint 7a)

**Status:** Accepted

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Rationale                                                | Alternative considered |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| The close opens a settlement case for the winner (open auctions and opened tenders) with deadlines from settings: agreement in 5 business days, balance or guarantees in 30 days (S2); steps recorded with date and an encrypted document; reminders at 7 and 2 days                                                                                                                      | Every sale has a tracked path                            | Manual follow-up       |
| Completion needs every step; an **applied** deposit moves to the price with a ledger "applied" entry; a **security** deposit is scheduled for refund (S5)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Money moves only through the ledger                      | Manual                 |
| Default only after a missed deadline; forfeiture capped at **the lowest of 10% of the price, the actual auction cost entered, and the deposit held** (shown with its arithmetic); security deposits need an explicit sign-off that forfeiture is approved under the sale terms; a **different person** confirms (auctioneers declare, finance confirms by default) (S3)                   | PRD cap; security deposits never forfeited automatically | Automatic forfeiture   |
| Confirmation: ledger "forfeited" entry, any excess scheduled for refund, buyer's approval revoked, **suspension** (12 months, setting) that blocks applying and bidding, then the runner-up is offered the property at **their own highest bid** with an acceptance window (S4); accepting cancels their scheduled refund and opens their case; decline or expiry lets the refund proceed | PRD default flow                                         | Re-auction only        |
| Mining outcome reads **"Preferred bidder: subject to approvals"** on the public page and in back office (S1)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | A winning bid does not transfer a mining right           | —                      |
| **The live feed can never fail a bid or a close**: broadcasts are dispatched after commit inside try/catch and reported. Found in the visual check: with Reverb down, the close crashed after recording the result but before the deposit hand-off, which a re-run would have skipped                                                                                                     | WebSockets are a convenience; pages fall back to polling | Synchronous broadcast  |
| **Crash-safe close**: each result records `handed_off_at`; `auctions:close` completes any unfinished hand-off (settlement opens once; duplicate refunds are refused); verified on the real stuck listing                                                                                                                                                                                  | No bidder stranded by a crash, deploy or outage          | Manual repair          |
