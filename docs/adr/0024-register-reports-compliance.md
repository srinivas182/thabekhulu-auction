# ADR 0024 — Investor register, financial reports and suspicious activity (Sprint 8c)

**Status:** Accepted

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Rationale                                               | Alternative considered    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Investor register** (`buyers.view`): one row per buyer with entities, verification, approvals, bids, wins and suspension; **KYC officers** also see screening and the last 4 digits of the ID (decrypted only for that), **finance** also sees deposits held; exports need `reports.export`, use the same per-role columns and are audit-logged (T5)                                                                                                       | POPIA: each role sees what it needs                     | One full view             |
| **Financial reports** (`reports.view`) for any period from the ledger, invoices and payouts: received, refunded, forfeited, applied, adjustments, held at period end, fees, VAT, invoiced, paid to sellers; checks that refunds paid equal the ledger's refund entries and that the all-time Sprint 5c reconciliation balances; ledger, invoice and payout CSVs (T6)                                                                                         | The accountant gets reconciled figures                  | Spreadsheet only          |
| **Suspicious-activity queue** (`compliance.review`, KYC officers and super admins): an hourly `compliance:scan` gathers signals already recorded elsewhere, each **once** (fingerprint): integrity flags (6c), non-clear screening (4b), data-room downloads at the 4c `daily_download_alert` threshold, bank lines unresolved for 3+ days (5a), confirmed defaults (7a), 3+ sign-in lockouts a week (audit log), and bid codes guessed wrongly 5 times (6a) | One place to review; no new detection logic to maintain | Separate pages per source |
| People decide: open, under review, dismissed, escalated; **dismissing or escalating needs a recorded reason**; the decider is stored; an **evidence pack** (item, notes, and the audit trail for the person and listing 30 days either side) is exported as JSON. **The platform never files reports**: suspicious transaction reports go to the FIC through Thabekhulu's compliance officer (T7)                                                            | Human judgement, documented                             | Automatic reporting       |
